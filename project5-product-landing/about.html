ABOUT / A PLACE TO PLAY

Every table has a story.

From collectible cards to cooperative adventures, gaming has a way of bringing people together. That's what makes a neighborhood game store worth celebrating.

THE STORE

Made for players and collectors.

Gilded Fates Cards & Games is a locally owned gaming and hobby store in Emporia, Kansas. The store focuses on tabletop games, collectible cards, and the people who enjoy them.

Gilded Fates opened in the Flinthills Mall area in 2025 and has been described as a place both to shop and to sit down for a game. That combination matters: it gives beginners a place to ask questions and experienced players somewhere to meet others who share their interests.

Products and organized-play schedules may change, so visitors should check with the store before making plans around a specific item or event.

WHY I CHOSE THIS BUSINESS

A local store with a hobby I already enjoy.

I chose Gilded Fates for this project because tabletop games have been a hobby of mine for a long time. A place that sells games is interesting, but a place where people can actually get together and play them is even better.

I wanted the website to show both sides of the business: the products on the shelves and the community that can develop around them. I also liked the challenge of designing a site for a real local shop rather than an imaginary product.

My goal was to create something easy to navigate on a phone or a computer, with enough information for someone new to gaming to feel comfortable visiting.
