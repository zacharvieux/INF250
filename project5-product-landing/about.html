ABOUT MY LOCAL BUSINESS PICK

Why I Chose Gilded Fates

A local business connected to a hobby I already enjoy.

THE BUSINESS

About Gilded Fates

Gilded Fates Cards & Games is a locally owned game and hobby store in Emporia, Kansas. The store carries trading cards, board games, tabletop games, miniatures, and other hobby products while also providing space for people to play.

What makes a local game store different from simply ordering something online is the community around it. Players can find something new, talk with people who understand the hobby, and have a place where they can actually play.

WHY I CHOSE IT

Gaming Is Already Part of My Life

I chose Gilded Fates because tabletop gaming is already a hobby that I enjoy. I play games such as Dungeons & Dragons and enjoy both the creativity and social side of tabletop gaming. Because of that, Gilded Fates stood out to me when I needed to choose a local business for this project.

Building a website around something I already understand also gives me a chance to create something that feels more personal instead of choosing a business simply because it meets the assignment requirements.

WHAT I LOVE ABOUT IT

It Creates a Place to Play

What I like most about Gilded Fates is that it offers more than products sitting on shelves. Having a local gaming store gives people a place to meet others who share the same interests.

I also like that the store supports several different parts of the hobby. Someone may be interested in trading cards, roleplaying games, board games, or miniature gaming and still find something that interests them.

WHO SHOULD VISIT?

You Don't Have to Be a Hardcore Gamer

I think Gilded Fates is worth visiting for experienced gamers, collectors, and people who are simply curious about the hobby. Trading card players can look for cards and other players, tabletop gamers can find products for their games, and someone completely new can see what different games are available.

It can also be a good place for someone looking for a social hobby. Games are designed to bring people together, and having a local place to play makes it easier to find other people who enjoy them.
