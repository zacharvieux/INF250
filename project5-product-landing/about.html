About Gilded Fates and why I picked it for my web development project.

About Gilded Fates

Gilded Fates Cards & Games is a local gaming store in Emporia, Kansas. It focuses on cards, tabletop games, and giving players a place to get together.

The store opened in 2025. I liked that it wasn't only about selling games; the community part of it was what made it stand out to me.

Why I Chose This Business

I have enjoyed tabletop games for a long time, so I wanted to work on a project that was connected to something I'm actually interested in.

It was also a chance to try building a site for a real local business. I worked on keeping the navigation simple and making sure the layout could fit on smaller screens.

Everyone Starts Somewhere

Whether you've been playing card games for years or have never opened a tabletop rulebook, there is something fun about finding a game you enjoy.
